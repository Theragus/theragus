**What I do**

- ☁️ **Enterprise Azure deployments.** Landing zones, hub-and-spoke, Terraform, Infracost *before*
  someone discovers Premium SSDs. Built to survive the person who built it.
- 🏢 **Enterprise M365 rollouts & migrations.** Exchange, Teams, Intune, Purview, Entra ID. Thousands
  of mailboxes, zero "have you tried logging out and back in".
- 🔗 **Graph API & REST APIs.** I speak fluent `@odata.nextLink`, I have opinions about delta queries,
  and I've made peace with HTTP 429. Retry-After is not a suggestion.
- 🔧 **Automation as a personality trait.** If I do it twice, it becomes a module. See
  [FabricTools](https://github.com/Theragus/FabricTools) — ~211 cmdlets so nobody has to click through
  the Fabric portal again. Also [mcp-nomad](https://github.com/Theragus/mcp-nomad) and other small
  helpers that do the boring parts while I take the credit.
- 🔐 **Secrets & platform plumbing.** Vault, Consul, etcd. Nothing lives in a document called
  `Passwords_FINAL_v3.xlsx`.
- 🧩 **Business problems > IT problems.** Half of "we need a new tool" is a process with two steps in
  the wrong order. I find them, I swap them, nobody buys a tool.

Every locked door in an enterprise tenant is really just a permission you haven't been granted yet.
Most of consulting is figuring out which one.
